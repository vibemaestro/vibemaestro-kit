# Compact instructions

When you summarise this conversation, keep these exactly, as written, before anything else:

- **Owner requests:** every request the owner made in this conversation that is not yet a request item (`R`) of the brain, in
  the owner's own words, verbatim, with where it was said and any due day.
- **Goals and focus:** every goal and every focus the owner named, in the owner's own words.
- **Owner decisions:** every decision the owner stated, in the owner's own words, with where it was said; every anchor in it
  (code words, PR numbers, paths) unchanged.
- **Delegation windows:** what the owner allowed without asking, and until when (absolute time with zone).
- **Open pull requests:** number, head SHA (7 hex), draft or ready, CI state; never a title or a body.
- **Running tasks:** the tasks still running (a `local-parallel` subagent, for example), each with its pull request.
- **Timers:** every wake-up or check that was scheduled, with its time.
- **Now and next:** the task in hand and the next steps, with their pointers.
- **Evidence:** every rule seen to hold or to break that is not yet in the brain's ledger (`brain op held|broke`), with its
  pointer and what differed, and every drift brought to the owner with the option the owner chose.

Drop tool output, file listings and reasoning that led nowhere first. The left brain (`## Brain · left brain`: goals,
decisions, windows) and the right brain (`## Brain · right brain`: focus, requests, the work) are re-injected after the
compaction by the vibemaestro-brain plugin, with a review card to handle first; the summary must not contradict them.
